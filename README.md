# 📌 Project Overview

Banking institutions process millions of financial transactions every day, making fraud detection and customer risk assessment one of the most critical challenges in financial services.

This project presents an enterprise-style Banking Risk & Fraud Analytics Platform that analyzes large-scale banking transaction data to uncover fraudulent activities, detect suspicious customer behavior, identify balance anomalies, evaluate transaction risks, and generate executive-level business intelligence.

The solution combines Python-based data processing with Power BI interactive dashboards to transform raw banking transactions into actionable insights for fraud investigation and strategic decision-making.

---

# 🚀 Business Problem

Traditional fraud monitoring systems often struggle to identify sophisticated fraud patterns hidden within massive transaction volumes.

Banks require a centralized analytics solution capable of:

- Detecting fraudulent transactions
- Monitoring suspicious customer behavior
- Identifying abnormal balance movements
- Measuring fraud impact
- Understanding fraud trends
- Prioritizing high-risk customers
- Supporting fraud investigation teams
- Enabling data-driven business decisions

# 📌 Key Insights

- Fraud is highly concentrated within **TRANSFER** and **CASH_OUT** transaction types.
- High-value transactions exhibit a greater probability of fraudulent activity.
- Significant balance deviations are strong indicators of suspicious transactions.
- A relatively small proportion of transactions contributes disproportionately to overall fraud losses.
- High-risk customer accounts can be identified using transaction behavior and balance movement analysis.
- Fraud patterns become more visible when transaction type, amount, and balance anomalies are analyzed together.
